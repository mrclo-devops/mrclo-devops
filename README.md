### 🛡️ About Me & Engineering Philosophy

I am a **Senior Integration & DevOps Engineer** specializing in distributed systems reliability, incident response, API architectures, and cloud-native automation. My core focus is bridging the gap between business operations and high-availability infrastructure:

- 🔒 **Secure by Default:** Zero-trust principles, signed SSH/GPG commits, automated secrets prevention, and hardened container configurations.
- ⚡ **Production Support & SRE:** High-severity incident triage, Linux systems troubleshooting, API diagnostics, and systematic root-cause mitigation.
- 🐳 **Cloud-Native & Containers:** Docker, Kubernetes (K3s/k3d), GitOps workflows, and resilient CI/CD pipelines.
- 🔁 **Resilient Automations:** Self-hosted workflow orchestration (n8n), microservices integration, and multi-vendor hybrid AI pipelines.
